Franchise Opportunity Profile

Visiting Angels

Visiting Angels is a non-medical home care franchise founded in 1998 in Bryn Mawr, Pennsylvania, operating as Living Assistance Services, Inc. Every fact on this page is sourced from the company's 2026 Franchise Disclosure Document (issued April 20, 2026), with Item and page citations.

Key Facts

Initial Franchise Fee

$64,950

Standard 200K-population Protected Territory; $51,950 (up to 100K pop) to $89,950 (325K pop). (FDD Item 5, p.12)

Estimated Initial Investment

$125,460 to $171,150

$51,950 to $89,950 of that is payable to the franchisor or affiliate. (FDD cover, p.1; Item 7, p.25)

Royalty

3.5% of Gross Revenues

Drops to 3.25% after $125,000/month and 3.0% after $225,000/month; tiered monthly minimums apply from the 2nd month. (FDD Item 6, p.13)

Cooperative Advertising

$425/mo or 2.5%

$425/month (or 2.5% of gross revenues if higher); 2.25% after $150K/mo; 2.0% after $250K/mo. (FDD Item 6, pp.13-14)

Territory

Protected, not exclusive

Protected Territory sized by population tiers; you will not receive an exclusive territory and may face competition from other franchisees. (FDD Item 12, p.49)

Term

10 years

Initial franchise term of ten (10) years. (Exhibit A — Franchise Agreement)

Training

~5 days

Initial training approximately 5 days (required for owner/principal shareholder). (Exhibit A — Franchise Agreement)

Transfer Fee

$9,500–$25,000 (or 2.5% of sale price, if greater)

Minimum transfer fee tiered by sale price of the business. (FDD Item 6, p.18)

Financing

Not offered

The franchisor does not directly or indirectly offer financing. (FDD Item 7, p.25; Item 10, p.36)

HQ

Bryn Mawr, Pennsylvania

Living Assistance Services, Inc., 937 Haverford Road, Suite 200, Bryn Mawr, PA 19010. (800) 365-4189. (FDD Item 1, p.8)

Leadership

Lawrence Meigs, President/CEO

President/CEO since September 1998; leadership team includes operations and development SVPs. (FDD Item 2, p.10)

System Size

541 outlets

541 franchised outlets at end of 2025; 0 company-owned; net +2 in 2025. (FDD Item 20, p.66)

Item 19 — Financial Performance

The FDD's only financial performance representation is a 2025 Revenue vs. Longevity Chart based on actual annual revenues reported by 550 franchisees that operated in calendar year 2025. Rows are revenue ranges; columns are months since the franchisee attended training. There were 541 franchisees operating at year-end 2025, 7 opened during 2025, and 9 closed during 2025.

  • 508 of 550 agencies (92%) had been operating 73+ months since training
  • 9 agencies over $10 million in 2025 revenue · 16 at $7-10 million · 20 at $5-7 million
  • 38 agencies at $4-5 million · 53 at $3-4 million · 37 at $2.5-3 million
  • 105 agencies at $1-1.5 million · 36 at $500K-$750K · 23 under $250,000
  • Confidence: MODERATE — full-system revenue coverage, but revenue-only figures with no profit, expense, or per-outlet identity data.

Risk Profile & Disclosure

23 / 100 risk score

Assessment: Lower. A 28-year franchising system with a large, stable outlet base (541 outlets, net +9 since 2023), low capital intensity, modest ongoing fees (6.0% combined), no bankruptcy, and audited financials — tempered by five disclosed litigation matters and a protected-but-non-exclusive territory model.

Risk inputs (documented):

  • Litigation: five disclosed matters in 10 years (two pending, one $5M settlement, two franchisor-v-franchisee) — pushes score up
  • Growth stagnation: net +9 outlets over 2023-2025 — pushes score up
  • Territory: protected but not exclusive; metro-area client crossover permitted — pushes score up
  • Ongoing fees: 3.5% royalty + 2.5% ad fund (6.0% combined, with volume discounts) — pushes score up modestly
  • Capital intensity: investment ceiling $171,150 — low — pushes score down
  • Item 19 breadth: 550 franchisees in the revenue-vs-longevity chart — pushes score down
  • Audited financials attached (Exhibit B); no bankruptcy disclosed — push score down
  • System longevity: franchising since 1998 (28 years) — pushes score down
